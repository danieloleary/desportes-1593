# Contributor routing

- README.md owns entry points. CONTRIBUTIONS.md owns claim scope.
- `showcase/` is buildless. Run `node --test showcase/tests/source-integrity.test.cjs`.
- `contrib/saved-score/AGENTS.md` and METHOD.md own the scoring checks.
- Preserve upstream code, readings, evidence, images, and licenses.
- Do not import/run acquired recognition scripts or load checkpoints without the
  applicable execution review. The owned reader and scorer do not need them.
- Keep source strings as DOM text, uncertainty visible, and credentials out of
  client assets. No private Site export, raw notes, or new archive images enter
  public commits without applicable publication and rights decisions.
- Keep this branch separate from the decoder PR. No force-push, upstream merge,
  author/archive outreach, or access change is implied.
